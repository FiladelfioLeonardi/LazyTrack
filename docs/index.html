Mac

macOS 12 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel

⬇ Download for Mac
  1. Unzip it, then right-click Install Mochi → Open.
  2. On newer macOS: open it once, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Mochi opens when it's done. Make your account and pick your habits.

Questions

Is Mochi a Tamagotchi app?

Kind of: Mochi is a Tamagotchi-style virtual pet, but instead of pressing buttons to feed him, your real habits do it. It's also a full habit tracker and screen time tracker for your computer, free on Mac and Windows.

Does it track my screen time automatically?

Yes. Mochi counts time in apps and websites by itself (coding, YouTube, social media, games), so you never have to log it. Other habits, like cigarettes, water or the gym, take one tap.

Can Mochi help me quit smoking or doomscrolling?

It makes every cigarette and every hour of social media visible, and Mochi pays for each one in HP. You set a daily limit for each bad habit, and Mochi can lower it by about 10% a week as you get better. It's not a medical program, just a very motivating pet.

How is it different from Finch or other habit pet apps?

Most habit pets live on your phone and wait for you to check boxes. Mochi lives on your computer and tracks your screen time by itself, so he reacts even when you forget to log anything. Friends, a wardrobe and a handheld egg are on top.

Where does my data go?

Your tracking data stays on your computer. Only what you choose to share with friends (like your Mochi's look and the numbers you picked) goes to the friends server. More in the privacy policy.

Does it watch my screen?

It only notices which app or website is in front, never what's on it. The optional smoking detector is explained in The camera, honestly: it runs only on your computer, only if you turn it on.

Can Mochi die?

His hearts drop on bad days, and yes, he can run out. Then a baby hatches and you start a new generation. Good days and your friends keep him going.
