Download LazyTrack

Free for Mac and Windows. It installs everything it needs, starts with your computer and updates itself.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11

Download for Windows
  1. Open LazyTrack-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. LazyTrack opens when it's done. Make your account and pick your habits.

Mac

macOS 12 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel

Download for Mac
  1. Unzip it, then right-click Install LazyTrack → Open.
  2. On macOS 15: open it once, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. LazyTrack opens when it's done. Make your account and pick your habits.

Questions

Where does my data go?

Your tracking data stays on your computer. Only what you choose to share with friends (like your Mochi and today's numbers you picked) goes to the friends server.

Does it watch my screen?

It reads which app or website is in front, never what's on it. The optional smoking detector uses your camera only on your computer, and only if you turn it on.

Can Mochi die?

His hearts drop on bad days, but there's always a way back. Good days, meals on time and your friends bring him back up.
